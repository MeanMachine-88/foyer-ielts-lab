Chart Room · describe, compare, ask

Read the data.
Then question it.

Three visuals about how people in a fictional city travel to work. First describe what you see. Then ask your partner questions about the data, like a curious reporter.

About 8 minutes · 3 stages
Your taskLine graphBar chartTableQuestion startersSelf-check

Your task, in three stages

  1. Stage 1 · Describe (about 2 min). Give an overview of the line graph: what is the biggest change, and what stays almost the same?
  2. Stage 2 · Ask (about 4 min). Ask your partner at least five different questions about the three visuals. Try "how much", "which", "compared with", and "what would happen if".
  3. Stage 3 · Explain (about 2 min). Your partner asks you to explain the pattern and give a personal opinion.
Start with: “Hello, I am ready for the Chart Room.”

Question starters

NumbersHow much did … change between … and …?
CompareWhich way is cheaper than … but faster than …?
TrendWhen did … start to rise faster?
ReasonWhy do you think … fell so much?
DetailWhat does the table say about …?
What ifWhat would happen if the bus cost less than USD 2?

Self-check after your session

  • Did my overview name the biggest change before giving details?
  • Did I use at least three numbers from the visuals?
  • Did I use comparison words: higher than, twice as, the least?
  • Were my five questions different from each other?
