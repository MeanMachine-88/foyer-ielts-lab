 Speaking Parts 2 & 3

Cue Card + Discussion

One minute to prepare, up to two minutes talking, then a deeper discussion about the topic.

How a session works

  1. Open a room and look at the page for a few seconds. Everything your partner talks about is on the page.
  2. Press the voice button and say hello. Allow the microphone when the browser asks.
  3. Speak in full answers. Your partner waits for you. If you freeze, say "Can you repeat that?" or "Can you give me a hint?"
  4. Listen to the feedback at the end: one thing you did well, one thing to fix, and a sentence to try again.
  5. Go again. A second attempt of the same room is the fastest way to improve.
