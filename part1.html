Speaking Part 1 · Interview

Short questions.
Real answers.

Your examiner asks about three everyday topics. Aim for two to three sentences each: answer, give a reason, add a small example.

4–5 minutes · 3 topics · 10–12 questions
How to answerTopic: HomeTopic: StudyTopic: Free timeLanguage bankWhat examiners listen for

The A-R-E pattern

AAnswer
Say it directly: “Yes, I do.” “I usually…”
RReason
“…because it helps me relax.”
EExample
“For instance, last weekend I…”
Start with: “Hello, I am ready for the interview.”
Topic 1

Home

Your partner will ask three or four questions on this topic.

  • Where do you live now? Is it a house or an apartment?
  • What is your favourite room, and why?
  • Is your neighbourhood quiet or busy?
  • Would you like to live somewhere else in the future?
Topic 2

Study

Three or four questions about learning and work.

  • What are you studying, or what do you do for work?
  • What do you enjoy most about it?
  • Do you prefer studying alone or with other people?
  • How do you usually practise English?
Topic 3

Free time

Three or four questions about relaxing and hobbies.

  • What do you like to do in your free time?
  • Do you prefer relaxing at home or going out?
  • Has your free-time activity changed since you were a child?
  • Is there a new hobby you would like to try?

Language bank

Buying thinking time: That’s an interesting question… / Let me think… / Well, I’d say…
Giving reasons: The main reason is… / That’s partly because…
Examples: For instance… / A good example is…
Preferences: I’d rather… than… / I’m quite keen on… / I can’t stand…
Frequency: every now and then / almost every day / once in a blue moon

What examiners listen for

SkillGood sign
FluencyYou keep going; few long pauses
VocabularyTopic words, not only “good / nice”
GrammarA mix of simple and longer sentences
PronunciationClear word stress and rhythm

Your partner gives feedback on these four areas at the end.
