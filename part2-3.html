Part 2

Your cue card

Describe a place where you like to study or read.

You should say:

  • where this place is
  • what it looks like
  • what you usually do there

and explain why this place is good for studying or reading.

Start with: “Hello, I am ready for Part 2.” Your partner will tell you when to start the timer.

Preparation timer

The real test gives you one minute to prepare, and you may make notes. Then you speak for up to two minutes.

01:00

Ready.

Your notes

Keywords only, not full sentences. Notes stay in this browser.

Long-turn map

  1. Open: “The place I’d like to talk about is…”
  2. Where: location and how you found it
  3. Look: two or three details you can see
  4. Do: a typical visit, step by step
  5. Why: two clear reasons, then a closing line
Part 3

Discussion topics

Your partner chooses questions from these two themes and asks follow-ups.

Theme A · Places for learning

  • How have places for studying changed in the last twenty years?
  • Should schools spend more money on libraries or on technology?
  • Do people learn better in quiet places?

Theme B · Technology and study

  • Is online study as effective as studying in a classroom?
  • What are the risks of studying with a phone nearby?
  • How might studying look different in fifty years?

Self-feedback checklist

  • Did I speak for nearly two minutes in Part 2?
  • Did I answer all four points on the card?
  • In Part 3, did I give opinions with reasons, not only facts?
  • Did I use however, whereas, as a result at least once?
